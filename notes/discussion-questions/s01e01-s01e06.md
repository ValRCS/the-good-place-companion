# Discussion after the first six episodes

> Viewing boundary: **S01E06** inclusive. The questions are philosophical prompts, not assertions that the series explicitly endorses a doctrine.

1. **Moral change:** Can Eleanor develop virtue through kind actions before her motives are entirely unselfish? Compare [S01E02](../episodes/season-01/s01e02.md) and [S01E03](../episodes/season-01/s01e03.md).
2. **Scoring lives:** Does Michael's score from [S01E01](../episodes/season-01/s01e01.md) measure merit, consequences, or something else? What about luck and intention?
3. **Chidi's burden:** What does Eleanor owe Chidi when being taught ethics costs him time and attention? Compare [S01E05](../episodes/season-01/s01e05.md) with [S01E06](../episodes/season-01/s01e06.md).
4. **Two moral theories:** Could one action maximize total happiness yet violate a moral principle an affected person could reasonably reject?
5. **Two students:** How should one teach ethics differently to Eleanor and Jason? Is knowing what is right enough to do it?
