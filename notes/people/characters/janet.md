# Janet

> **Character profile restricted to first-appearance knowledge (S01E01).** Later developments belong in individually bounded episode notes, not this global profile.

**Initial role:** The neighborhood's seemingly all-knowing information and assistance interface.

## Introduction

Janet is introduced as a source of facts and practical assistance within the Good Place. Her presence establishes a comic contrast between seemingly unlimited information access and the characters' much more difficult problems of ethical judgment.

## Ethical significance (interpretive)

An information source can help retrieve facts without settling what action ought to be taken.
