# Trevor

> **First-appearance knowledge only:** through S01E08. Later behavior is documented in episode-specific records, not this profile.

**Initial role:** A representative of the Bad Place who arrives to collect Eleanor after Michael investigates her mistaken placement.

## Introduction

Trevor arrives with a Bad Place group and treats the matter with visible cruelty and mocking enthusiasm. His confidence, discourtesy and pleasure in humiliating others sharply contrast with Michael's gentler approach. At the end of the episode Trevor announces that another Eleanor Shellstrop has been held by the Bad Place.

## Philosophical significance (editorial)

Trevor provides an accessible example of cruelty as enjoyment of another's suffering, rather than mere negligence or lack of manners.
