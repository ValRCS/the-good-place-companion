# Eleanor Shellstrop

> **Character profile restricted to first-appearance knowledge (S01E01).** Later developments belong in individually bounded episode notes, not this global profile.

**Initial role:** The viewpoint character, who discovers that her account of a virtuous life does not match the life she remembers.

## Introduction

At her arrival in the Good Place, Eleanor realizes she has been mistaken for a much more accomplished person. She chooses to confide in Chidi and seeks help rather than immediately announcing the error. The contrast between survival and moral improvement is already visible, but the outcome of that struggle is not presupposed here.

## Ethical significance (interpretive)

Eleanor has a strong instinct for self-preservation and considerable difficulty trusting the elaborate moral credentials attributed to her.
