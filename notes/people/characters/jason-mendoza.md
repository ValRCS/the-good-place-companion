# Jason Mendoza

> **Character profile restricted to first-appearance knowledge (S01E04).** Later developments belong in individually bounded episode notes, not this global profile.

**Initial role:** The man introduced as the silent monk Jianyu, whose real identity becomes available in Episode 4.

## Introduction

In S01E04 the seemingly silent monk is revealed as Jason Mendoza, a DJ from Jacksonville with a past very unlike the one assigned to him. His difficulties maintaining the disguise add a second case of misidentification to the story.

## Ethical significance (interpretive)

Jason's impulsiveness and limited inclination to deliberate create a contrast with Eleanor's strategic approach to her situation.
