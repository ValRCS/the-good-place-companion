# Uzo

> First-appearance profile; read after S01E10.

Chidi's longstanding friend appears in flashbacks. He becomes frustrated when Chidi's prolonged deliberation interferes with ordinary activities and commitments. His role dramatizes the interpersonal burden of indecision rather than establishing that all hesitation is morally wrong.
