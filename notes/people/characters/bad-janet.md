# Bad Janet

> **First-appearance knowledge only:** through S01E09.

**Initial role:** A counterpart to the Good Place's Janet who accompanies the visiting Bad Place group.

## Introduction

Bad Janet appears with behavior and an attitude notably different from the Good Place assistant. Her introduction extends the contrast between the two afterlife environments to their information-and-service interfaces.

## Philosophical significance (editorial)

If two apparently similar artificial assistants exhibit different attitudes, what should observers infer about programming, disposition, moral motivation, experience and agency? Behavior alone does not conclusively establish subjective experience.
