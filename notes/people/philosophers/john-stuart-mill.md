# John Stuart Mill (1806–1873)

British philosopher, political thinker and major nineteenth-century exponent of utilitarianism.

## Central ideas

Mill's utilitarian ethics evaluates actions by their contribution to happiness and the prevention of unhappiness. In *Utilitarianism* he distinguishes differences in the quality of pleasures and responds to objections about justice, motivation and the place of moral rules.

## Important distinctions

Mill developed Jeremy Bentham's earlier utilitarian tradition but did not merely reproduce it. 'The greatest good for the greatest number' is a classroom shorthand, not a substitute for Mill's full treatment of welfare, justice and higher pleasures.
