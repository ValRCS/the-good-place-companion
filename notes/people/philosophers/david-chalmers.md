# David Chalmers

Philosopher of mind associated with the “hard problem” of consciousness: why and how physical and functional processes are accompanied by subjective experience.

## Central distinction

Being able to explain a system's verbal and behavioral functions may not explain what, if anything, it experiences. The philosophical dispute is open; the distinction is not itself a proof about any particular machine.
