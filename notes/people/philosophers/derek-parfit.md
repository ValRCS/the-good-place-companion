# Derek Parfit (1942–2017)

British philosopher of ethics and personal identity. *Reasons and Persons* (1984) examines rationality, concern for the future and relations of psychological continuity.

## Important caveat

Parfit's work questions the philosophical importance of some assumptions about identity; it does **not** imply that ordinary changes of character make someone a different legal or moral person free of their past responsibilities.
