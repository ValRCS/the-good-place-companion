# G. W. F. Hegel (1770–1831)

German philosopher associated with German idealism. His account of self-consciousness in *Phenomenology of Spirit* includes the role of recognition and social relations in the development of self-understanding.

## Recognition

Hegelian recognition is not simply a desire to be liked. The relevant intellectual problem concerns self-consciousness, freedom and relations between persons who acknowledge one another.
