# T. M. Scanlon (born 1940)

American moral philosopher widely associated with a distinctive form of contemporary contractualism.

## Central ideas

Scanlon grounds an account of interpersonal morality in principles that no person could reasonably reject as a basis for informed, unforced general agreement. Reasonableness of an objection is crucial; he does not offer a rule that any participant can veto for any personal preference.

## Important distinctions

Scanlon's theory is moral contractualism, not simply a theory of the origin of states or political legitimacy. It differs from straightforward total-welfare maximization because it asks what reasons individuals can offer for rejecting proposed principles.
