# Thomas Nagel

Philosopher whose essay “Moral Luck” (1979) challenges the apparent tension between moral blame and conditions beyond an agent's control.

## Central ideas

Nagel distinguishes consequential or resultant, circumstantial, constitutive and causal forms of moral luck. One should not infer from this taxonomy that an agent is thereby excused for intended wrongdoing.
