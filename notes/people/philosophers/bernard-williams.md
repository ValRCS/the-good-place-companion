# Bernard Williams (1929–2003)

British philosopher who questioned overly schematic moral theories and explored responsibility, ethical integrity, and the role of outcomes in practical justification.

## Moral luck

His influential essay “Moral Luck,” collected in the book of the same title (1981), considers how an agent's evaluation of a choice can be shaped by its eventual consequences.
