# Alan Turing (1912–1954)

British mathematician, logician and computer-science pioneer. His 1950 paper “Computing Machinery and Intelligence” proposes the imitation game as a way of approaching questions about machine intelligence and humanlike linguistic behavior.

## Important distinctions

The imitation game addresses evidence for intelligence through interaction. Passing a behavioral test is not in itself a proof of phenomenal consciousness or entitlement to moral rights.
