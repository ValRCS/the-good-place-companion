# Søren Kierkegaard

## Intellectual background

Søren Kierkegaard (1813–1855) was a Danish philosopher and religious writer.

## Central ideas

His pseudonymous works examine existential choice, anxiety, inwardness and commitment. Distinguish their literary voices from a single systematic doctrine; subjective commitment does not make every ethical claim correct.

## Primary works

Either/Or (1843); The Concept of Anxiety (1844).
