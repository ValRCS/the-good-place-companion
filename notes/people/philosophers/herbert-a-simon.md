# Herbert A. Simon

## Intellectual background

Herbert A. Simon (1916–2001) was a scholar of organizations, economics, cognition and artificial intelligence.

## Central ideas

Bounded rationality treats decision-making as constrained by time, knowledge and processing ability. Satisficing is a search strategy based on an acceptable threshold, not a general moral license to ignore constraints.

## Primary works

Administrative Behavior (1947); Models of Man (1957).
