# Aristotle (384–322 BCE)

Greek philosopher whose ethical writing examines flourishing, excellence of character, deliberation, and the good human life.

## Central ideas

In the *Nicomachean Ethics*, moral virtues are cultivated through habituation: good actions and emotional education help form stable dispositions. Virtue is not merely following a rule or maximizing a numerical reward. Practical wisdom (*phronēsis*) matters to discerning what a situation calls for; Aristotle's doctrine of a mean is relative to us and governed by reason, not a mechanical midpoint.

## Important distinctions

A single generous act does not by itself prove that someone is generous in character. Likewise, the popular slogan that virtues are habits is only a starting point: Aristotle also requires trained perception, appropriate feeling and practical wisdom.
