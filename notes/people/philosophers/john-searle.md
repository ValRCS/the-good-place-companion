# John Searle

American philosopher known for philosophy of language, mind and intentionality. His Chinese Room argument challenges the claim that executing a formal program, by itself, suffices for understanding.

## Central clarification

The thought experiment focuses on **understanding and intentionality**; it should not be simplistically cited as a demonstration that any artificial system necessarily lacks consciousness, feelings or rights.
