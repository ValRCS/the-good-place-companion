**Focus:** Eleanor and Michael's points system.

## Quick synopsis

Eleanor Shellstrop awakens in an apparently idyllic afterlife neighborhood. Its architect, Michael, tells her that her exemplary earthly conduct has earned her a place there. Eleanor knows that he has described somebody else's life: she appears to have arrived by mistake. She confides in Chidi Anagonye, the ethics professor assigned as her soulmate, and asks him to help her remain. Strange disruptions make her fear that her presence is somehow upsetting the neighborhood.

## Main story and character developments

- Michael introduces an elaborate system that awards and subtracts points for earthly actions.
- Eleanor conceals her mistake but seeks help from Chidi.
- Janet is introduced as a source of seemingly limitless information; Tahani's accomplishments contrast with Eleanor's background.

## Central ethical dilemma

Can a person deserve eternal reward or punishment on the basis of a numerical assessment of their actions? What matters besides the action's result?
