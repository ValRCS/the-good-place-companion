**Focus:** Jason's identity and responsibility.

## Quick synopsis

The person presented as the silent monk Jianyu explains that he is actually Jason Mendoza, a DJ from Jacksonville who has been assigned someone else's identity. His impulsive behavior makes maintaining the disguise difficult. Eleanor and Chidi try to manage the risks of disclosure, particularly during a public restaurant event where a misguided distraction creates further chaos. Chidi is drawn into the challenge of helping both Eleanor and Jason.

## Main story and character developments

- Jason's background contrasts sharply with the reserved role assigned to him.
- Eleanor learns that the neighborhood contains another person who does not match the expected biography.
- Chidi faces competing demands from two students with very different dispositions.
