**Focus:** Utilitarianism and Chidi's burdens.

## Quick synopsis

The growing neighborhood sinkhole creates an emergency that brings strangers temporarily into Eleanor and Chidi's home. Chidi grows exhausted by having to teach Eleanor and Jason, while Eleanor is initially more concerned with her own success than with his workload. Tahani learns that her points total is low compared with other people in the neighborhood, unsettling her assumptions about status and goodness. Eleanor begins to grasp the need to consider what her requests cost Chidi.

## Main story and character developments

- Chidi's ethics instruction explicitly reaches utilitarianism.
- The emergency places Eleanor and Chidi under additional social pressure.
- Tahani's ranking produces a crisis of comparison and self-worth.
