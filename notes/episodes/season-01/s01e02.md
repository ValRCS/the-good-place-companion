**Focus:** Chidi's ethics instruction and Eleanor's motives.

## Quick synopsis

Eleanor tries to follow Chidi's advice while the neighborhood prepares for a chance to fly. Chidi proposes that she do something unglamorous and generous instead, by helping with a cleanup. Eleanor's attempt to retain personal advantages while appearing to volunteer causes trouble. Through the experience, she begins to confront the difference between being seen to do good and actually caring about others. Meanwhile, the pressure of keeping her secret intensifies.

## Main story and character developments

- Chidi moves from discussion of philosophical readings to the practical demand that Eleanor help her neighbors.
- Eleanor faces a direct choice between enjoying the flying opportunity and doing useful work.
- A threatening message increases her anxiety about being discovered.

## Central ethical dilemma

If an action benefits others but is performed for selfish reasons, is it morally good?
