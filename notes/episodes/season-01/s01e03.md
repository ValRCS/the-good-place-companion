**Focus:** Tahani, Eleanor, envy, and kindness.

## Quick synopsis

Eleanor's suspicion of the apparently flawless Tahani collides with Chidi's first practical lesson: be kind to one's neighbor. As she gets to know Tahani, Eleanor confronts her own jealousy and learns that outward appearances do not settle the question of someone's character. Michael helps Chidi look for a more rewarding pursuit than endlessly revising his vast philosophy manuscript. At the episode's close, the person known as Jianyu reveals to Eleanor that he, too, has a secret.

## Main story and character developments

- Eleanor's relationship with Tahani begins to change from rivalry to a more sympathetic understanding.
- The 3,600-page manuscript becomes a comic illustration of Chidi's intellectual preoccupation.
- The supposed silent monk Jianyu unexpectedly speaks to Eleanor and admits he does not belong.
