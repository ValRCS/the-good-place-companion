# *Nicomachean Ethics*

- **Author:** [Aristotle](../people/philosophers/aristotle.md)
- **Date:** fourth century BCE (composition and arrangement debated)
- **Genre:** Philosophical treatise in ten books; the title is conventionally associated with Aristotle's ethical writings.

## Central argument and relevant sections

Human flourishing (*eudaimonia*), the cultivation of character, voluntary action, practical wisdom, friendship and the relation of virtue to pleasure. Book II is especially useful for the discussion of habituation.
