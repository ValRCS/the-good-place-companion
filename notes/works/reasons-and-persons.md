# *Reasons and Persons* (1984)

- **Author:** [Derek Parfit](../people/philosophers/derek-parfit.md)
- **Publication:** Oxford University Press, 1984.
- **Relevant part:** Part III, *Personal Identity*.

## Key relevance

Examines survival, psychological continuity and whether personal identity has the moral importance often attributed to it. Do not infer from the theory that every meaningful character change literally creates a new individual.

- [SEP — Personal Identity](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/identity-personal/)
