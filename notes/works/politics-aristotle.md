# *Politics* — Aristotle

- **Author:** [Aristotle](../people/philosophers/aristotle.md)
- **Approximate date:** fourth century BCE; textual transmission and composition complex.
- **Relevant part:** Book I, especially the discussion of human beings as political animals.

## Key relevance

Treats human community and the polis as constitutive matters for political and ethical life, rather than treating human flourishing as simply solitary pleasure. For ethical habituation also consult Aristotle's [*Nicomachean Ethics*](nicomachean-ethics.md).
