# *The Conscious Mind* (1996)

- **Author:** [David Chalmers](../people/philosophers/david-chalmers.md)
- **Book:** *The Conscious Mind: In Search of a Fundamental Theory* (Oxford University Press, 1996).

## Key relevance

Argues that explaining cognitive functions does not straightforwardly explain the existence of subjective experience. Provides background for distinguishing an entity's behavioral responses from the difficult question of what, if anything, it feels.
