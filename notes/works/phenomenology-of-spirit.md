# *Phenomenology of Spirit* (1807)

- **Author:** [G. W. F. Hegel](../people/philosophers/georg-wilhelm-friedrich-hegel.md)
- **Original title:** *Phänomenologie des Geistes*
- **Relevant part:** the section on self-consciousness and recognition.

## Key relevance

Provides an interpretive perspective on self-consciousness, otherness and reciprocal recognition. Its argument is not reducible to a psychological need for compliments or group approval.

- [SEP — Hegel](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/hegel/)
- [SEP — Recognition](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/recognition/)
