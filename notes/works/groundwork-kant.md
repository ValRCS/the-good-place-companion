# *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785)

- **Author:** [Immanuel Kant](../people/philosophers/immanuel-kant.md)
- **Original title:** *Grundlegung zur Metaphysik der Sitten*.
- **Relevant sections:** I–II; the moral law, duty and formulations of the categorical imperative.

## Key relevance

Provides background for duty and the requirement to treat rational persons as ends in themselves. The *Groundwork* is distinct from Kant's later *Metaphysics of Morals* (1797). A general discussion of lying does not prove that this particular book appears on screen.
