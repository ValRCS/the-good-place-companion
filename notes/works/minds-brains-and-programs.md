# “Minds, Brains, and Programs” (1980)

- **Author:** [John Searle](../people/philosophers/john-searle.md)
- **Publication:** *Behavioral and Brain Sciences* 3(3), pp. 417–424 (1980), followed by peer commentary.
- **DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1017/S0140525X00005756

## Central issue

Presents the Chinese Room thought experiment to question whether syntactic manipulation under a program suffices for semantic understanding. Does not directly settle whether particular AI systems can suffer or deserve moral consideration.
