# Symposium

Plato, fourth century BCE; a dialogue presenting different speeches about eros. Aristophanes' myth of divided beings (189c–193d) gives a vivid account of longing for wholeness. It must not be conflated with Diotima's later ascent toward appreciation of beauty (210a–212a) or treated as Plato's unqualified endorsement of one predestined partner.
