# Virtue ethics

**In brief:** A family of ethical approaches centered on character, practical wisdom and human flourishing.

## Explanation

Aristotelian virtue ethics emphasizes stable excellences such as courage, justice, generosity and temperance. Virtues arise through education, repeated practice and the cultivation of appropriate feelings and judgment. The ultimate account of a flourishing life (*eudaimonia*) is wider than a momentary pleasurable outcome.

## Important distinctions

Unlike action-first theories, virtue ethics often begins with the question of what kind of person one should become. It does not claim that following habits mechanically is enough: practical wisdom must guide the appropriate action in particular circumstances.

## A question worth debating

Can an apparently selfish person become genuinely virtuous through repeated acts of kindness?
