# Moral motivation

**In brief:** What moves a person to act for moral reasons, and how the motive relates to the value of the action.

## Explanation

An agent might comply with a moral rule out of fear, hope of praise, empathy, duty, habit, or thoughtful commitment. An identical outward action can have different motives. The philosophical issue is not simply whether the behavior is helpful but how moral judgments, reasons and desires can motivate conduct.

## Important distinctions

Distinguish the rightness of an action from the moral worth of its motivation. Kant emphasizes acting from duty; Aristotle's ethics considers the development of dispositions through practice. The two traditions do not give an identical account of motivation.

## A question worth debating

Can practice undertaken for selfish reasons eventually transform a person's character? How would we distinguish authentic change from strategic performance?
