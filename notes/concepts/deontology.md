# Deontology

**In brief:** Ethical theories that assign fundamental importance to duties, permissions, rights or constraints.

## Explanation

A deontological approach does not regard the best aggregate consequences as the sole determinant of right action. Kantian ethics, a major example, asks whether the maxim of an action can be morally justified and insists that persons must be treated as ends rather than merely as means.

## Important distinctions

Deontology is a family of views, not a single algorithm or a synonym for Kant alone. Duties can conflict, and philosophical accounts differ over how constraints, obligations, and exceptions operate.

## A question worth debating

Could a duty to be truthful or to respect another person still bind when dishonesty might produce a better immediate outcome?
