# Veil of ignorance

## Explanation

The original position restricts knowledge of one's particular identity, talents, wealth, beliefs and social circumstances while retaining general facts. It models fairness in choosing principles for institutions; it does not erase individual identities in real life.

## Discussion

What information should an impartial constitutional designer know?
