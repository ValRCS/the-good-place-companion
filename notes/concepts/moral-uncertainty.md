# Moral uncertainty

## Explanation

Moral uncertainty concerns which evaluative principles are correct or how they apply. Factual uncertainty concerns what is true or likely. Preference uncertainty concerns what one or others want. Analysis paralysis can occur under any of these, and even with no serious moral stakes. The presence of competing reasons does not establish that distinct moral theories are equally credible.

## Discussion

How much deliberation is justified before acting under uncertainty?
