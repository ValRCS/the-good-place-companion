# Practical wisdom

## Explanation

Aristotelian practical wisdom is judgment about what a concrete situation requires. Continuing to deliberate may consume resources, miss deadlines and burden others. Inaction is not always wrong, but it does not automatically escape moral evaluation. For Aristotle, phronesis requires trained perception, virtuous character and reasoning about particulars.
