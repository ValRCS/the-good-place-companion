# Bounded rationality

## Explanation

Herbert Simon emphasizes the limits of information, time and computational capacity. Instead of exhaustive optimization, a decision-maker can search until a reasonable aspiration threshold is met. A satisficing rule does not remove obligations or authorize arbitrary choices where rights are at stake.

What would a defensible stopping rule look like for a time-limited choice?
