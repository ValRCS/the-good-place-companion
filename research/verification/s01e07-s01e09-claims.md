## Categories

- **Directly verified:** a viewing with version/time or exact first-party material establishes the claim.
- **Secondary corroborated:** at least one external recap or guide reports it; can still be mistaken.
- **Interpretive:** philosophical comparison or argument produced for the companion.
- **Unverified:** no sufficient evidence for the literal attribution.
