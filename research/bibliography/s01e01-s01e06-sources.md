# Bibliography: Episodes 1–6

> The following works are scholarly background, not necessarily books shown in the episodes.

## Primary philosophical sources

- Aristotle. *Nicomachean Ethics*, especially Books II (habituation) and VI (practical wisdom). Select an edition and translation before quoting.
- Kant, Immanuel. *Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals* (1785) and *The Metaphysics of Morals* (1797). **Distinct books**; the one shown in S01E02 is not yet confirmed.
- Mill, John Stuart. *Utilitarianism* (serial publication 1861; book publication 1863), especially Chapters II and V.
- Scanlon, T. M. *What We Owe to Each Other*. Harvard University Press, 1998.
