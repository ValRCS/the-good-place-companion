Season 1 · Episode 1

Everything Is Fine

What does it mean to deserve a good place—and can someone deliberately become better?

Spoilers through S1E1 onlyStarter recap
Read after viewing Episode 1.

No events from later episodes appear here.

Brief recap

Eleanor Shellstrop arrives in an apparently ideal afterlife neighborhood. Michael welcomes her and explains that she earned her place through an exemplary life. Eleanor realizes that the account of her past does not match her actual life. She turns to Chidi Anagonye, a professor of ethics introduced as her soulmate, and asks him to help her become a better person.

Original summary. Scene-level references will be added during the rewatch.

Philosophical questions

Moral desert

Should a reward depend on actions, intentions, character, or outcomes? This is an interpretive theme, not a claim that the episode explicitly names the theory.

Can character change?

Eleanor's request for instruction suggests a question central to virtue ethics: can moral habits be taught?

Why study ethics?

Chidi's profession introduces ethics as a discipline of reasoned moral inquiry. See our ethics primer.

References and verification

No philosopher, book, or quoted passage is attributed to this episode without specific verification. The themes above are editorial interpretations.

Discussion questions

  1. What should Eleanor do after recognizing the mistake?
  2. Would a good deed motivated by fear of punishment still be morally good?
