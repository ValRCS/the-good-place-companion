Read safely

Our spoiler policy

A page about an episode never assumes you have watched the next one.

One page, one boundary

An episode page may describe events only through the end of its named episode. The limit is stated prominently. Future season indexes contain no episode-level details before publication.

Not even hidden

Never put later plot information into earlier pages—even inside collapsed HTML, page titles, metadata, search excerpts, alt text, or link labels. Hidden content can still be indexed and accidentally disclosed.

Standalone philosophy

Philosophy essays may explain ideas without revealing later narratives. Future retrospective analysis, if included, belongs on its own clearly labeled page with a minimum viewing boundary.

Protect two viewing experiences

The companion is being created alongside a first-time viewer of Season 1 and a returning viewer who has not finished the final season. Do not presume knowledge of subsequent developments.

Contributor rule:

If a detail foreshadows, reinterprets, or reveals a later episode, omit it from the earlier page. Do not publish a spoiler in a public research note simply because it is outside the Pages website.
