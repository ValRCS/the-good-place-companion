How the notes are made

Editorial standards

Original criticism and accessible philosophical study, not a transcript mirror or unsourced fan wiki.

Three kinds of connections

  1. Explicit reference: A philosopher, theory, work, or other reference actually named or shown. Record the scene or timestamp where practical.
  2. Interpretive connection: The editor's analysis; do not present it as an explicit reference or as evidence of authorial intent.
  3. External context: Scholarly, historical, or production facts with traceable sources.

Source quality

Prefer primary philosophical texts, academic scholarship, and reputable encyclopedias such as the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy. Record editions and translations when wording matters.

Originality and rights

Write original recaps. Avoid reproducing extensive dialogue, subtitles, transcripts, images, and artwork. Attribute brief necessary quotations and external factual claims.

Review before publication

Mark outlines clearly, verify reference claims, and check links, accessibility, and spoiler boundaries before publishing.
